Book review

Grokking Algorithms

In Aditya Y. Bhargava’s diagrams, you can watch binary search discard half the possibilities at each step. That gives big-O notation a concrete example before you reach the code.

Mohamed Halawa/22 Nov 2026

Aditya Y. Bhargava gives you something to look at while you work out what an algorithm does. With binary search, you can follow the shrinking search space before reading the implementation. By the time big-O notation appears, it has an operation you can picture.

The graph examples stood out to me. In breadth-first search and Dijkstra’s algorithm, the drawings let you follow the order in which the search visits nodes. In the dynamic programming chapter, the grid did something similar. Each cell gives a smaller problem a place on the page, so you can trace the work that goes into the next answer.

My November 18 reading note covers the chapters I’d reached. I was still working through the second half when I wrote it.
