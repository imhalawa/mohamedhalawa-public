About Mohamed Halawa

I’m a backend software engineer at Coolblue. I work with C# and .NET on systems that handle payments. When a service publishes an event, another service has to decide what that event tells it and what to do next. That handoff is the part of software architecture I like writing about.

A similar question came up in TechJobsNL, my vacancy tracker. A job missing from a complete scan can be marked closed. A job missing because the scanner timed out should keep its previous state. The distinction shapes what the database can tell someone a week later. Read the case study.

I also build tools for editing notes and watching services after deployment. You can explore the projects, read my résumé or get in touch.
