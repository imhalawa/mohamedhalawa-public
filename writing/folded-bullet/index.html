Why a folded bullet lost its place in Obsidian

Roamer drew an outline guide in the right place until someone folded the item. The fix came from measuring the second render.

Mohamed Halawa/22 Nov 2026

Fold a callout in Roamer and its body disappears, leaving the title in the outline. Unfold it and the body comes back. In an earlier version, that round trip could leave the vertical guide in the wrong place. You could open the same note from scratch and see it drawn correctly.

Roamer is my outliner for Obsidian. A callout inside a list has two parts with different owners. Obsidian draws the callout, including its icon and title. Roamer positions it beside the bullet and draws a line connecting that bullet to the next one. The alignment depends on geometry that only exists after Obsidian has laid out the row.

What a fold changes

The text can stay exactly the same while the editor changes the style on a rendered line. When Obsidian reapplied that style during a fold, it cleared the offsets Roamer had written there. The guide still existed. Its position was wrong.

Open the note  →  Roamer measures the row and positions the guide
Fold the item  →  Obsidian reapplies the line style
Unfold it      →  Roamer needs fresh measurements for the open row

Adding a fixed offset would have helped one arrangement of bullets, theme and font size. The fix was to measure again when the editor folds or unfolds a range, and when a callout line loses its layout properties. That lets the editor finish laying out the actual row before the plugin positions its guide.

What the test measures

The end-to-end test opens a note in Obsidian, folds a callout, then unfolds it. Checking that the guide exists would have let the bug through again. The test now compares its horizontal position with the center of the bullet, allowing 1.5 pixels of difference. It also checks that the guide starts at or below the bottom of the bullet and that the note still contains its original Markdown.

That last check matters to someone using the plugin. They can open the note in another editor after a day of folding and unfolding it. All those view changes should leave them with exactly the text they wrote.
