After trying tmux and Zellij, I settled on herdr. I can use the mouse and keyboard habits I already have, which leaves more of my attention for the task in the terminal.

Mohamed Halawa/12 Sept 2026

I use herdr with Ghostty on my Mac and Windows Terminal on Windows, both natively and through WSL. It earned its place on every machine I use by letting me keep working without having to remember another set of shortcuts.
