About this field guide

I’m Mohamed Halawa. I write this field guide for people who like to know why a piece of software behaves the way it does. A note can start with something small. In one case, a bullet in Obsidian lined up perfectly until I folded it and opened it again. Following that one movement led to a walkthrough of how the editor lays out a line.

This site gives me room to explain a decision that might take one sentence in a README. I link to the code or change behind each piece, so you can follow the evidence yourself. The projects page collects the tools those decisions came from.

My résumé covers my professional background. If a piece reminds you of a problem you’ve worked on, get in touch.
