Colophon

I keep the source notes in an Obsidian vault. A build step selects the Markdown files I’ve marked for publication and passes them to Astro. GitHub Pages serves the generated HTML. This lets me keep a rough sketch beside the finished article and publish the article on its own.

PT Serif sets the titles and article text. DM Sans sets the navigation and labels. Both fonts are hosted with the site. The home page diagram follows one vacancy through a scan to show when its saved state can change.
